# Event Floor Rules
(A Request to Everyone)

Let's all follow these rules so we can have fun and play safely together!

## ■ 1. To Everyone Participating in the Event
● Remember to be polite and considerate toward your opponents and the staff, and have fun playing until the very end.
● Follow the rules of the event and matches, and compete fairly and honorably.

## ■ 2. Items Used in Matches
● Please ensure that your Character Chips and Energy Chips comply with the event rules (regulations).
● Except for Energy Chips customized using officially released custom chips, modified or altered Character Chips and Energy Chips may not be permitted for use in the event.
● Please store any items not being used in the match in your bag or similar container, and do not leave them on the table.
● Use cards with text that is clearly legible. Cards that are torn or dirty, or cards in sleeves where the text is difficult to read or illegible, cannot be used in matches.

## ■ 3. Rules During Matches

● Be sure to clearly announce the moves and effects you’re using: Announce the moves you’re using and the effects of Character Roll in a voice that your opponent can clearly hear, and in a way that’s easy to understand.

● How to Roll Character and Energy Balls: When rolling Character or Energy Balls, make sure to roll them properly so they move smoothly.

● Throwing Is Prohibited: Do not take a back swing to throw them or slam them down forcefully, as this can break the Character or Energy Balls or hit people nearby, posing a safety hazard. Please refrain from doing this under any circumstances.

## ■ 4. Ending a Match
● If the match time runs out, please continue the match until the player whose turn it is to go second has finished their turn.
● After that, compare your HP; the player with the higher HP wins.
● If your HP is tied, play rock-paper-scissors to determine the winner.

## ■ 5. When You Have a Problem or Need to Leave Your Seat

● Please be sure to follow staff instructions at all times during the event.

● If you’re unsure about the rules or encounter a problem during a match, do not make a decision on your own; instead, pause the game and call a nearby staff member.

● If you absolutely must leave your seat during a match (e.g., to use the restroom), be sure to let a staff member know. At that time, please leave all items used for the match on the table.

● If you need to leave the event early, be sure to inform a staff member before leaving.

## ■ 6. Watching Matches and Taking Photos/Videos

● Etiquette for Watching Matches: Please refrain from making comments that might distract the players or offering advice on their next moves, and watch quietly.

● Posting Photos and Videos on Social Media: You may take photos inside the venue, but please absolutely refrain from posting photos or videos that show other people’s faces on the internet (such as social media) without their permission. When posting to social media, please use stickers or other methods to obscure faces so they are not visible.

## ■ 7. Regarding Event Staff Conduct

● If staff members are asked questions about the match details, they should refer to the “How to Play” page or the Q&A section on the official website, and proceed with the event accordingly.

● If staff members discover any rule violations, cheating, or suspicious behavior, they should, at their discretion, point them out or take corrective action.
